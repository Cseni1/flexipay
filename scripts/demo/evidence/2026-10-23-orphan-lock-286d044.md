The released payout was locked with the 50 bps fee (49134282) while the frozen
quote said 100 bps (98268564). Claim accept checks only that the settle leg is
`processing` with refs; it does not know a drift occurred, because CCR-15 step 2
did not run. This is the concrete consequence of leaving recovery open: the
payment can still become `paid`, just not through `/fund`.

## `Payment.lockAttempt` vs `Leg.attempt`

- `Payment.lockAttempt` is the vault lock-attempt counter. It derives the
  external id `sha256("<payment id>:<attempt>")` and is the value CCR-15 uses
  for `findLock(payer, paymentId, attempt)`.
- `Leg.attempt` is the provider attempt counter (CCR-14: one retry, increment on
  retry). The settle path does not advance it for a lock attempt.
- In this run both are **1** for the settle leg, and the old defect's
  `lockAttempt=2` / `Leg.attempt=1` divergence no longer occurs on replay
  because replay does not bump either counter.
- The counters still mean different things. If a genuine new lock attempt were
  made, `lockAttempt` would advance while `Leg.attempt` stayed at 1; CCR-15 keys
  recovery to `lockAttempt`, CCR-14 names `Leg.attempt` as the durable source of
  truth. The owner should state which one describes a settle payout retry.

## Handoff

CC2 (Csaba/Cursor) still needs CCR-15 step 2: on mismatch, mark the settle leg
`failed` with a durable error, keep refs, call `refund_vault(payoutId)`, and

it is neither failed nor refunded, and a claim accept can still pay it.
